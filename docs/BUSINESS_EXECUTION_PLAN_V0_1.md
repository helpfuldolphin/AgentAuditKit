# AAK Business Execution Plan v0.1

Status: Active internal plan  
Objective: Convert AAK into repeatable cashflow while preserving Lane B scope discipline

## Strategy

AAK is sold as a deterministic decision-lineage and evidence primitive for high-liability AI workflows.

Do not sell as:

- compliance certification
- legal guarantee
- governance authority engine

## Critical Path Sequencing

## Phase 0 (Now -> Giuseppe Call)

Primary focus:

- MathLedger clean repo waves (A0-A4)
- Three-lane experiment pipeline (A5-A7)
- Preliminary results + call preparation

AAK scope in this phase:

- maintain release quality
- ship claim-language hygiene
- ship scoring-provenance contract scaffolding
- no broad outreach campaign yet

## Phase 1 (Immediately After Call, 30 Days)

Primary outcome: close one lighthouse engagement.

Actions:

- build a 40-account ICP list (regional banks/credit unions first)
- execute 5 high-quality targeted outreaches per week
- run discovery calls and push fixed-scope pilot close
- close 2 paid audits (target), minimum 1 lighthouse

## Phase 2 (Days 31-90)

Primary outcome: transition from one-off audits to recurring revenue.

Actions:

- convert audit findings into remediation sprint offers
- convert remediation into evidence assurance retainers
- publish anonymized case evidence with measurable deltas

## Offer Ladder

- Tier 1: AI Decision Exposure Audit (`$2,500`)
- Tier 2: Expanded Audit + onsite readout (`$5,000`)
- Tier 3: Remediation Sprint (`$7,500-$15,000`)
- Tier 4: Evidence Assurance Retainer (`$2,000-$4,000/mo` or annual equivalent)

## ICP and Buyer Roles

Priority ICP:

- regional banks / credit unions
- insurance operators
- healthcare billing processors
- legal operations teams using AI automation

Buyer roles:

- CTO
- CISO
- head of risk/compliance
- internal audit lead

## Sales Question (Default)

"If a wire, lending, or other AI-influenced decision is challenged next quarter, can you reconstruct the exact prompt chain, tool calls, and influence context with tamper-evident evidence?"

## Architecture Requirements for Revenue Credibility

Must-have:

- deterministic replay and fail-closed verification
- deterministic narrative report output
- psych scoring provenance contract and versioned classifier metadata
- explicit synthetic vs live labeling in reports

Do-not-add during this cycle:

- dashboards
- hosted SaaS
- compliance certification claims
- broad feature expansion

## KPI Targets

Phase 1 KPIs:

- 5 targeted outreaches/week
- 4+ discovery calls/month
- 1-2 paid audits/month

Phase 2 KPIs:

- 30%+ audit-to-remediation conversion
- 20%+ remediation-to-retainer conversion
- first referenceable lighthouse case

## Integration Flywheel (Commercial + Research)

Each real engagement should produce:

- paid revenue
- case-study evidence for sales
- anonymized empirical data for research

## Risk Controls

- change messaging before changing architecture
- maintain non-claims discipline in all artifacts
- freeze scope when active pilot delivery is underway
- require deterministic evidence before making any new outward claims
