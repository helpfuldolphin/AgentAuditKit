\noindent\textbf{Audience:} Regional bank CTO/CISO, risk, internal audit, and technology leadership.

\section*{Executive Summary}
Most institutions now have AI-assisted workflows in operations, support, underwriting, fraud review, or internal analysis. The operational risk is not only model quality. The larger risk is \textit{decision exposure}: if an AI-linked decision is challenged, can your team reconstruct what happened in a defensible way?

\medskip
\noindent Many teams can produce logs. Fewer can produce a replayable, tamper-evident decision lineage that stands up under incident response, legal review, or regulator scrutiny.

\section*{The Core Gap}
\textbf{Logging} answers: ``what events were recorded?''\\
\textbf{Defensible lineage} answers: ``can we reproduce the exact path of prompts, tool calls, and outputs, and prove artifacts were not silently altered?''
\newline
\textit{Including multi-turn psychological convergence patterns (authority + urgency + social proof) that traditional logs miss.}

\medskip
\noindent In practice, the gap appears when teams must answer:
\begin{itemize}
  \item Why did this automated action occur?
  \item Which external tools and data sources influenced the outcome?
  \item What evidence is missing today if this decision is disputed?
  \item Could we reproduce this review path tomorrow with confidence?
\end{itemize}

\section*{What We Do}
We provide a fixed-scope \textbf{AI Decision Exposure Review} for one selected workflow.

\begin{itemize}
  \item Map one real workflow where AI influences a material decision.
  \item Capture and reconstruct decision lineage using deterministic replay methods.
  \item Identify irreversible actions, external side effects, and evidence gaps.
  \item Produce a concise, executive-readable exposure report with concrete next steps.
\end{itemize}
\noindent\textbf{Reference scenario baseline:} Validated in deterministic synthetic CEO-fraud and Command Authority Confusion scenarios matching financial services attack patterns. Live workflow validation is conducted per-client during engagement.
